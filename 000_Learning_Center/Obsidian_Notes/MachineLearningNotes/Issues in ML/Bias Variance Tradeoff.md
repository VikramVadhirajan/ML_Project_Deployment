# Bias Variance Tradeoff

The bias variance tradeoff explains the balance between **model simplicity and model flexibility**.

---

## Bias

Bias is the error caused by **simplifying assumptions in the model**.

High bias leads to **underfitting**.

Example:  
Using a linear model for nonlinear data.

---

## Variance

Variance measures how much the model prediction changes with different training datasets.

High variance leads to **overfitting**.

Example:  
Very complex models that memorize training data.

---

## Tradeoff

Increasing model complexity:

- decreases bias
    
- increases variance
    

A good model balances both.
