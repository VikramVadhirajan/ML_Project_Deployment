# Overfitting

Overfitting occurs when a model learns **noise in the training data instead of the true underlying pattern**.

As a result, the model performs well on training data but poorly on unseen data.

---

## Causes

- Excessively complex models
    
- Too many features
    
- Small datasets
    

---

## Solutions

- [[Regularization]]
    
- More training data
    
- Simpler models
    
- Cross validation
