# Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

Mean Absolute Error measures the **average absolute difference between predicted and actual values**.

Unlike [[Mean Squared Error]], it does not square the errors.

---

# Formula

MAE = (1/n) Σ |yᵢ − ŷᵢ|

---

# Intuition

MAE measures the **average size of prediction errors** without emphasizing large errors.

Example:

MAE = 5 → predictions are off by about 5 units on average.

---

# Advantages

- easy to interpret
    
- less sensitive to outliers compared to MSE
    

---

# Limitations

- does not strongly penalize large errors
