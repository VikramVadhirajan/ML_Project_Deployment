# Recall

Recall measures the **proportion of actual positive cases correctly identified by the model**.

It focuses on detecting as many positives as possible.

---

# Formula

Recall = TP / (TP + FN)

---

# Interpretation

High recall means:

The model **captures most of the true positive cases**.

Example:

Disease detection where missing a positive case is dangerous.
